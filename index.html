About me

I work on the philosophy of science, with a focus on social epistemology, epistemic trust, and the conditions under which collective scientific knowledge becomes possible. My research examines how trust, collaboration, and institutional structures shape the epistemic output of large-scale research projects.

My doctoral project, Epistemology of Trust: How Trust Shapes Knowledge, investigates how trust contributes to the formation of reliable knowledge in scientific collectives, with particular attention to projects such as (ng)EHT and LIGO. I also engage in science communication and public outreach through my volunteering work at the Osservatorio Astronomico G.V. Schiaparelli.
